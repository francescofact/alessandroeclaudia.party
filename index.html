Level 15

The Dome Room

From inside the circular dome room and its 360° exterior balcony, take in sweeping panoramic views across Berlin. This spectacular space will host our sunset flying dinner and later transform into our dance floor under the historic cupola.

  • 360° panoramic view over the Berlin skyline
  • Wrap-around outdoor balcony for fresh air & sunset
  • Flying dinner & evening dance floor
  • Iconic 1950s monumental cupola architecture

Level 14

The Bar

A fully equipped designer cocktail bar high above the city — the perfect backdrop for signature drinks, conversations, and toasts. Our dedicated bartenders will keep the glasses full while our DJ sets the groove right next to the counter.

  • Full open bar with custom signature cocktails
  • Italian wines, champagne & Berlin craft beer
  • DJ booth & sound setup

As you arrive on the tower floors, Level 13 is your first destination: an intimate retreat with elegant ambiance, comfortable Chesterfield leather armchairs, our Polaroid photo corner, and wedding guestbook.

  • Plush leather seating & relaxed atmosphere

  • Welcome reception & coat check area
  • Quiet haven for conversations away from the dance floor

September 19, 2026

Our Celebration

After tying the knot in an intimate ceremony, there is only one thing left to do: raise our glasses, laugh, dance, and share a magical night together with all of you!

For this celebration, we chose one of Berlin's most iconic architectural landmarks: the monumental Henselmann towers at Frankfurter Tor, built in the 1950s at the entrance to Karl-Marx-Allee. The entire south tower is reserved exclusively for our party.

From sunset drinks on the open-air balcony to a continuous flying dinner and dancing late into the night under the dome — we cannot wait to toast with you above the sparkling lights of Berlin.

Exquisite Dining

Culinary & Drinks

Great food and wine are central to celebrating life. For our evening, we've curated a delicious flying dinner blending Italian roots with contemporary Berlin flavors, served continuously so everyone can mingle freely.

  • Aperitivo & flying antipasti at sunset
  • Handcrafted pasta & Mediterranean delicacies
  • Rich vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free selections
  • Wedding cake, artisan dolci & midnight bites
